# Contoso SupportHub X1 Troubleshooting Guide

**Scope:** This document provides fictional troubleshooting guidance for common SupportHub X1 errors.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-17

Error E17 indicates stale device enrollment. Restart the device, refresh the enrollment token, and retry the operation.

If E17 persists after two retries, escalate the issue to the support team with the error history and enrollment details.
